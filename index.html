Ratnesh Kr. Joshi
Post-Doctoral Researcher | LLM Safety, Explainability & Benchmarking

I am a Post-Doctoral Researcher at the Indian Institute of Information Technology Bangalore, with a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from IIT Patna. I specialize in building transparent, safe, and trustworthy Gen-AI systems through rigorous benchmarking and evaluation frameworks.

My research focuses on three critical pillars: (1) Explainable AI (XAI) and feature attribution for conversational systems, (2) LLM safety and hallucination mitigation through RAG and knowledge grounding, and (3) rigorous numerical and factual benchmarking to evaluate and improve model reliability. I develop domain-specific evaluation methodologies and statistical knowledge graphs to expose failure modes in frontier LLMs.

With proven expertise in RAG systems, model debiasing, negotiation dialogue, and agentic AI evaluation, I bring both theoretical rigor and industry experience from collaborations with Samsung Research Institute, Accenture, and Siemens Technology. My work directly advances the safety and interpretability of large language models at scale.

EcoStatKG: A Domain-Specific Statistical Knowledge Graph and Benchmark for Evaluating Numerical Hallucination in LLMs

Authors: Ratnesh Kr. Joshi, Sagnik Sengupta, and Asif Ekbal

Status: Under Decision at AACL (ARR) – AACL/IJCNLP 2026 Conference Submission

Key Contribution: Presents EcoStatKG (50,746 statistical triples) and NumHallu (500-question numerical benchmark) revealing that LLM numerical accuracy hinges on knowledge graph coverage, not retrieval sophistication. Shows cosine-similarity triple retrieval reaches 0.989 Numerical Exact Match (NEM) when gold triples are indexed. Demonstrates confidence-based gating suppresses hallucinations, restoring zero-shot performance levels.

CPRO: Concept Preference Reasoning Optimization

Authors: Ratnesh Kr. Joshi and Kshitij Mishra

Status: Under Review (ARR Track)

Key Contribution: Proposes framework that trains models to identify candidate solution concepts before reasoning through them. Explores multiple concept-conditioned reasoning strategies with novel rewards steering exploration from word-level to concept-level search. Reduces redundant reasoning over duplicate strategies and improves problem-specific exploitation. Achieves state-of-the-art results on GSM8K, MATH, and AIME benchmarks, demonstrating largest benefit on hard olympiad-level problems where strategic diversity is most valuable.

Nudging towards Sustainability: Persona-Driven Reinforcement Learning for Empowering Informative Conversation

Status: Under Review (ARR Track)

Key Contribution: Investigates chatbots as agents for driving sustainable behavior adoption. Creates EcoNudge dialogue dataset enabling understanding of diverse user personas' responses to sustainability-focused guidance techniques. Proposes persona-aware RL model to craft impactful interactions tailored to broad persona characteristics. Demonstrates efficacy of RL-finetuned language models outperforming well-prompted LLMs, showing feasibility of smaller models achieving comparable performance with proper reinforced fine-tuning.
